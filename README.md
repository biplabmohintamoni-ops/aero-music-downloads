## 📖 About

**Aero Music App** is a free music app for Android, built as a community project. It brings a simple, modern and smooth music experience: browse trending songs, search your favourites, follow artists, save songs to your library and enjoy synced lyrics.

🌐 **Official website:** https://get.aeromusic.workers.dev

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Home:** personalised greeting, recently played and trending songs
- 🔍 **Search:** find songs and artists quickly
- 🌍 **Multiple languages:** Hindi, English, Punjabi and more
- 🔥 **Trending:** see what everyone is listening to
- ▶️ **Player:** clean full-screen music player
- 📝 **Lyrics:** read lyrics while the song plays
- 📚 **Library:** keep all your music in one place
- ❤️ **Liked songs:** save the songs you love
- 🎤 **Artists:** explore artist pages and their songs
- 🎨 **Themes:** customise the look of the app

## ⬇️ Download & Install

1. Go to the [**Releases page**](../../releases/latest) and download the latest `Aero-Music.apk`.
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.
3. If Android asks, allow **Install from unknown sources** for your browser or file manager.
4. Tap **Install** and open **Aero Music**.

> 💡 You can also download it from the official website: https://get.aeromusic.workers.dev
